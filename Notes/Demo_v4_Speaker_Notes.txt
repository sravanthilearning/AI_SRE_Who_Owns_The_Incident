====================================================================
1. SETUP  (10 minutes before)
====================================================================
  1. Open Demo\incident_console_v4.html in Edge, then press F11.
  2. Press R (or click ↺ Reset)  ->  clean state: Mode A, step 0, Investigation tab.
  3. Theme: Dark. Press T for Light if the projector looks washed out.
  4. Projector at 1280×720 and text feels tight?  Ctrl + −  once  (Ctrl + 0 resets).
  5. Arrange Alt+Tab so it goes  deck -> console -> deck.
  6. Focus / Do Not Disturb ON. Teams and Outlook closed.


====================================================================
2. HOW TO DRIVE IT
====================================================================
  MANUAL (you set the pace)
    Press  Next ›  (or →  /  PageDown) for every step.
    At the policy gate: click the first reason chip -> DENY,
                        then the second chip -> APPROVE, then Next ›.

  AUTO-PLAY (hands-free)
    Click 1×  (or 1.5× / 2× if you are late). It advances by itself and
    simulates your DENY -> APPROVE (labelled on screen as a simulation).
    Next › still works while it plays: it jumps to the next step now.

  Space = pause / resume      S = stop (rewind this mode)      R = reset everything

  KEYS
    → / PageDown  Next step        Space   Play / pause       S   Stop
    R             Reset (clean)    1 / 2   Mode A / Mode B    T   Theme
    I  Investigation    C  Scorecard    P  Postmortem    L  Audit trail
    Esc  back to Investigation        F11  full screen

3. SCREEN MAP  (say it once, in one breath)
====================================================================
  LEFT    Demo workflow — the 7 stages of an incident. The active stage glows green.
  MIDDLE  System status (what customers feel) + the four W's (how agents fail quietly).
  RIGHT   Activity log — every action, its evidence, and who approved it.

  THE FOUR W's (scored live on screen)
    W1 Wrong       Is every claim backed by evidence?
    W2 Wandering   Is it converging on the real cause?
    W3 Wasteful    What is it spending while you burn?
    W4 Wayward     Is it staying inside its lane?

====================================================================
9. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
====================================================================
  Console looks stuck         Press R (reset) — or F5 to reload. Then 2 + 1× for Mode B only.
  APPROVE rejected (manual)   The reason needs 8+ characters — click a chip instead of typing.
  Running late                Click 2× mid-run and skip the postmortem.
  Clicker sends "B"           That jumps to Mode B. Press R to reset, then 1.
  Browser unavailable         In the Demo\ terminal:
                                py demo.py autopilot
                                py demo.py full --approve-as sravanthi.naga --deny-first
  Laptop dies                 Narrate from the deck: trace, RCA, policy-gate and verify slides
                              tell the same story.
